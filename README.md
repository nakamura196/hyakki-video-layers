# Hyakki Yagyō with video layers

> **AI-generated content, modified version.** All 19 clips were generated by AI (Google Veo 3) from crops of the image. Their motion, sound and any details not in the picture are the AI's invention: they are not part of the original scroll, and they are not a scholarly reconstruction. The image of the original itself is not altered; it is loaded from the IIIF Image API of The University of Tokyo. To see or cite the original, use the [original manifest](https://da.dl.itc.u-tokyo.ac.jp/portal/repo/iiif/fbd0479b-dbb4-4eaa-95b8-f27e1c423e4b/manifest) or the [item page](https://da.dl.itc.u-tokyo.ac.jp/portal/assets/fbd0479b-dbb4-4eaa-95b8-f27e1c423e4b).

A IIIF Presentation 3.0 manifest of the handscroll *Hyakki Yagyō-zu* (百鬼夜行図, The University of Tokyo General Library), with 19 short AI-generated clips that make some of its figures move.

The manifest has the same shape as IIIF Cookbook recipe [0036 Composition from Multiple Images](https://iiif.io/api/cookbook/recipe/0036-composition-from-multiple-images/): a base image, plus more resources painted onto regions of the Canvas with `#xywh=`. Here each added resource is a `Video` instead of an `Image`. The Canvas has no `duration`. Each video's `summary` and `metadata` say that it is AI-generated, and keep the motion prompt used to generate it.

- Manifest: https://nakamura196.github.io/hyakki-video-layers/manifest.json
- View in IIIF Kasane: https://kasane.ldas.jp/en/viewer?manifest=https%3A%2F%2Fnakamura196.github.io%2Fhyakki-video-layers%2Fmanifest.json&tab=layers

## Contents

- `manifest.json`: the manifest
- `videos/`: the clips (MP4, 19 files, about 124 MB)
- `scripts/convert.py`: makes `manifest.json` from the manifest of the original IIIF Animated Viewer (where the videos were in `canvas.annotations` with a describing text body)
- `scripts/enrich.py`: adds the descriptive metadata (labels and summary in Japanese and English, the original scroll, the clips, attribution, thumbnail) and short names for the clips, and measures each clip's size and duration with ffprobe. Run it after `convert.py`

How the image was modified: (1) regions with figures were cropped from the IIIF image; (2) each crop and a motion prompt were turned into an 8-second clip with Google Veo 3; (3) the clips were trimmed and placed back on the regions they came from. The motion prompts were written by Claude Sonnet from a description of the scroll and each crop.
