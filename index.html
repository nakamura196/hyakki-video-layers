Hyakki Yagyō with video layers

A IIIF manifest of the handscroll Hyakki Yagyō-zu (The University of Tokyo General Library) with 19 AI-generated clips painted onto regions of the Canvas, in the shape of IIIF Cookbook recipe 0036.

AI-generated content, modified version. The clips were generated by AI in Google Flow (Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash; Veo 3.1 Fast for 003, 011, 017 and 018, which are redrawn in a different style in between) from crops of the image. The edges and every part that does not move are the pixels of the original; only the moving parts come from the video. Their motion and sound are not part of the original scroll and are not a scholarly reconstruction. The image of the original is not altered. 動画はすべて AI が生成したもので、原資料には含まれません（『百鬼夜行図』（東京大学総合図書館所蔵）を改変）。
